# Crown Vault v21 — viewer ranges and richer treasure

Faith can save a username and minimum/maximum final total under **Username
ranges · private** in her control page. Enter that name in **Playing for** to
apply the range automatically. Matching trims spaces, ignores case and accepts
a leading @; it is an exact name match, not a substring or Twitch integration.
A matched range takes priority over the selected preset. Other names use the
selected preset. Rules persist for this vault in this browser until edited or
removed, including in settings backups and duplicates. Changes only affect
future rounds. The editor's optional Demo viewer previews the same matching
rule without changing a live round.

Ranges accept whole dollars from $1 to $1,000,000, inclusive, up to 100 names
per vault. Each range round selects an inclusive uniform whole-dollar final
target privately before starting, then constructs and validates a full sequence
of cash and bonuses through the normal award engine. It has five starting
chambers and one to three earned extra unlocks, a bonus in the first three
chambers, and optional stacking ×2 multipliers. Opening cash is 18–32% of the
pre-multiplier total, rounded down to whole dollars. Special chambers receive
higher allocation weights than normal chambers. Very small ranges may use
cent-sized cash awards so every chamber still adds something. The exact final
total is reached by ordinary additions and full multipliers, never a final
clamp or subtraction. These bounded rounds use their own planned draws, not
the custom editor's independent 35% bonus odds. Presets and Custom remain
unchanged when no username rule matches.

The accepted plan freezes in the saved round for reload recovery. Public
snapshots and the actual session publisher exclude the username table, active
range, selected experience and future prize plan. Only the entered player
name and revealed game results are shown on stream. Local controls receive
private metadata separately. This is browser-local configuration, not an
account-wide synced username directory.

Larger visible prizes add stepped gold glints, side fountains and warm brass /
celesta / glass accents. Cash tiers start at $150 and $300; deeper lock accents
follow $1,000 and $2,500 already banked. Finales add two fountain waves from
$1,500 and a third-wave encore from $4,000, aligning with the existing reveal
clock. The effects depend exclusively on revealed amounts, not the private
preset or viewer rule. They stay inside 420 × 460, keep the central amount
clear, honor reduced motion, and use the existing volume control. No opaque
background or extra overlay area. High and Extreme keep their guaranteed
ranges while receiving the stronger presentation as the winnings rise.

Vault sound palette includes Treasure lock accent, Grand cash flourish and
