# Gift Pile: four private total-range buttons

In **gifts.html**, set a name, minimum and maximum for each of the four games, then
choose **Save game**. Limits are whole dollars from $1 to $1,000,000; equal limits
produce an exact total. Both limits may stay blank until Faith is ready to set up
a button. No payout limits are assumed for existing saved games.

In **Open controls**, enter the viewer and press a game button. This reveals the
board; click one present at a time or use **Unwrap for me**. Buttons lock until
the current celebration clears. No Twitch command or chat connection is needed.
The editor also has four demo buttons; **Reset** makes another available.

The final total includes stacked multipliers and every bonus pick. A private
round plan allocates the cash before any gift is opened; it never clamps or
subtracts from the final result. Every displayed cash prize and multiplier adds
up exactly. Original bonus types, first-three-pick guarantee and board/pick caps
remain in effect; bonus eligibility/odds may adjust to fit a requested range.
Impossible custom combinations fail before a round starts. In range games,
cash values are generated automatically; saved individual cash values remain
available under Advanced for classic games with no configured ranges.

The selected game, all four ranges and unrevealed prize plan stay in the control
browser and are excluded from stream messages. Refreshing resumes the same plan.
Editing limits affects the next viewer, and version-4 settings backups retain all
four buttons while older backups still import. The transparent overlay and its
existing display link stay the same.
