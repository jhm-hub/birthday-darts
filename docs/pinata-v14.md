# Piñata v14 — adjustable royal celebration levels

The whole game's final total selects one of three celebrations:

- **Birthday win:** the existing crown, candy shower and warm birthday payoff.
- **Royal win:** a growing gold fan, extra coin confetti and a gold chime flourish.
- **Grand Royal:** jewels gather around the crown, with a second confetti burst
  and a separate jewel encore.

In the game editor, **Royal celebration levels** has two thresholds. Defaults
are Royal at **$1,500** and Grand Royal at **$5,000**, inclusive. Both use whole
dollars; Grand Royal must be higher, up to $1,000,000. Press **Save game** to
apply them to future games. Each active game freezes its starting thresholds,
including games with saved username ranges. These levels do not change prizes,
odds, multipliers, extra swings or guaranteed totals.

Three buttons preview Birthday, Royal and Grand Royal using the current values.
They play a one-hit celebration sample in the main preview, including sound
when enabled. Switching previews uses the normal farewell. Samples do not start
a live viewer game, consume a username rule, or change saved rewards. Unsaved
thresholds can be tried before saving.

The approved crown keeps its size. The overlay remains 420 × 460 and transparent.
Gold/jewel ornaments and extra confetti stay above or below the complete amount
and detail region; the amount's existing entrance, font fitting, hold and exit
are unchanged. Reduced motion suppresses added ornaments and particles. New
effects finish before the held result clears. Tier selection uses the revealed
total and frozen thresholds, never a private username range or future plan.

The new sounds use the existing mixer and cue scheduler, including mute,
late-join, reset and duplicate-play protection. The original sounds remain the
foundation. Combined peak checks at 44.1/48 kHz remain below −13 dBFS at full
gain. Browser auditions are available through **Sound on** and the tier buttons.

Art close-up scenes include each tier, a Royal flourish checkpoint and the
Grand encore. Refresh the editor, Faith's controls and OBS Browser Source to
load the new presentation together.
