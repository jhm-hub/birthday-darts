# Crown Vault v33 — coins into the next lock

Coins turn face-on over their last 75 ms, ease to a stop, and sink two pixels
into the bankroll edge while fading over 55 ms. The deposit light now holds
through the last arrival before easing into the guide departure. The number’s
small pulse, exact value and compact backing remain unchanged.

The guide takes 220 ms (previously 200), with one leading glint and two softer
followers. The next lock begins catching their light 30 ms before arrival,
reaches its held pose at the existing readiness time and carries that pose
through either an immediate click or a long pause. Three existing glints are
reused; no extra particle system or full-screen flash was added.

One 45 ms cabinet click replaces the sustained next-lock note, timed to guide
arrival. Payout ticks and the approved bonus/door/jackpot sounds are unchanged.
There is silence before the ready click and before the next key turn. The
palette’s Coins → next lock entry auditions the complete sparse bonus deposit
and lock-ready click. Ordinary cash-only deposits keep their existing ticks.

All game rules, guaranteed preset ranges, private viewer settings, manual
readiness, special vault audio, final celebration and reset clocks remain
unchanged. The overlay is still transparent at 420 × 460. Reduced motion keeps
static readouts and omits coins and guides.

Tests cover facing/settle continuity, number clearance, deposit-to-guide light
overlap, lock capture, immediate/paused clicks, exact sample clocks, bounded
mix levels and preview/live PCM equivalence. Existing privacy/range/finale
