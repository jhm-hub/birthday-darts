# Gift Pile: single bottom bar

Faith requested a cleaner stream presentation with unnumbered presents and one
information bar below the board. The approved present sprites are unchanged.

The bottom bar contains the viewer, picks remaining, an active multiplier and a
larger running total. Cash and bonus announcements share its left side while the
picks and total remain readable. Separate title/caption/footer boxes, board-size
labels and visible A1/B1 present IDs are removed. Accessible row/column names and
stable cell IDs still support keyboard selection and saved rounds.

The board, bonus traces and final-pick sparkle origin move together to leave room
for the bar. It remains readable through the finale, observes Royal anticipation
and fades with the existing round reset. Long usernames can use two lines. The
420 × 460 transparent Browser Source and private display links are unchanged.

Validation: 149 Gift Pile checks; browser review of ready, cash, multiplier, row,
growth, 5 × 5, 6 × 6, long-name, Royal anticipation and finale states on light/dark
backdrops; 252 × 276 viewing sample; paired control/display round with a selected
present, exact $101 total and automatic reset. Browser console errors: none.
No physical phone or OBS session was used for this review.
