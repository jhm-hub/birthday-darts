# Vault wins built through bonuses

High and Extreme use the same bounded planner as edited ranges and username
overrides. In that planner, targets of $1,500 or more build through:

- Five initial chambers and two or three earned special vaults (7–8 clicks).
- Two ×2 boosts (×4 total), or three (×8 total) for targets of $5,000+ that
  support exact-cent ×8 arithmetic. Odd whole-dollar totals use ×4.
- A modest opening balance, cash in every chamber, richer special-vault cash,
  and a multiplier in one of the final two special vaults.

The total is chosen inside the saved inclusive range, then split into actual
awards. For example, $10,000 comes from $1,250 collected and three ×2 boosts.
Cash generally uses $5/$25 increments; any exact-cent remainder belongs to a
real final cash award. There is no end-of-round adjustment or hidden subtraction.
The custom editor's 35% gate describes Custom settings, not the planned ranges.

Faith can watch **Watch $10,000 bonus run** in the Vault editor. It plays a full
disposable sample without saving settings, changing a live round or overriding
the selected game's range. Reset returns to the normal demo selection.

Default Low/Medium and manually configured Custom rounds retain their previous
rules. Current saved rounds keep their original frozen plans. New High/Extreme,
edited ranges and username rounds retain existing privacy, exact totals,
permanent OBS links, manual clicks, art, audio and timing.

Verification covers the actual engine path across exact, odd, narrow, tiny and
large ranges; bonus counts; late boosts; richer special cash; serialization;
range guarantees; public snapshot privacy; and preserving existing settings.
