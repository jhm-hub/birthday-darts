# Vault wins built through bonuses

Game 1 keeps its existing prize sequence. Games 2–4 now use smaller cash prizes,
extra vaults and stacked ×2, ×4 or ×6 total multipliers. The final chamber always
awards a multiplier on the whole accumulated bankroll, after adding its cash.
Larger username-range wins and the $10,000 demo use the same approach.

Five chambers start the round. Keys unlock the extra chambers before they are
needed, with a bonus guaranteed in the first three openings. New planned runs
have 8–15 openings; $7,500+ runs can reach 12–15. A round begins with cash alone,
then builds through 2–4 multiplier hits and earned extra vaults. The final boost
keeps its existing anticipation, readable equation and casino payoff.

Within the original four total ranges, ordinary chamber cash stays at $100 or
less and bonus-vault cash at $250 or less, with an opening balance no higher
than $150 in Games 2–4. The two pools remain separate. Very large edited exact
amounts can require larger cash if the target cannot divide into enough of the
available multipliers in whole cents. The saved total range always takes priority.

The target is still drawn with the existing lower-weighted distribution. Every
cash prize is positive and every multiplier is applied by the real game engine;
there is no end-of-round clamp or hidden adjustment. Only multiplier paths that
produce exact cents are eligible. For example, $10,000 can grow from $1,250 in
collected cash through ×2 then ×4, or three ×2 hits. ×6 finales appear when the
chosen total supports them. Names, ranges and unplayed prizes remain private.

the editor. Both use disposable, mathematically complete samples. Current saved
rounds keep their original frozen plans; the new sequence applies to new rounds.
Custom cash-pool games retain their configured multiplier and 35% bonus rules.
No saved version, private game name, range or permanent OBS link is replaced.

## The bonus-vault story

Each earned special vault gets a numbered chapter entrance: First Find,
Deeper Inside, Treasure Trail, Crown Reserve, then Inner Treasury. The jewel
burst grows with depth, while two small floating markers show the current
bonus vault and the multiplier already earned. They occupy the clear shoulders
of the existing 420 × 460 sprite. No target or private game name is displayed.

In later special chambers, a multiplier with an existing boost and at most two
unlocks remaining gets a 480 ms anticipation beat. Its badge gathers pixel
sparks, a short casino reel pickup leads into the existing multiplier sound,
and the calculation shows the banked amount before the boost lands. Coins,
commit, next chamber and finale all follow the same saved finish clock.
Ordinary chambers retain their pace. Old saved shots retain their old timing.

Special entrances rise subtly in register as the journey deepens, using the
same number of sound layers. The cash, anticipation, multiplier and coin
sounds stay separated; the total remains exact and readable throughout.

## Multiplier-to-jackpot handoff and display stability

The last late boost (including one in the penultimate chamber) carries its
multiplier seal from the bankroll into the crown. The final multiplier equation stays
visible through the jackpot strike; the receipt then explains the completed
round. Existing award, coin, celebration and clear deadlines remain unchanged.
The connecting casino phrase replaces the second wind-up, using two spaced
pickup notes before the established jackpot. Wide totals route the seal above
the plaque so the digits remain clear. Reduced motion disables the seal travel.

The editor's **Preview last boost + finale** button skips to the last chamber
of a disposable, mathematically complete $10,000 example. It changes no settings
or live round.
