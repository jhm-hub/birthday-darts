# Crown Vault — fresh arrivals and special bonus vaults (v17)

A small gold jewel leads into each fresh Crown Vault, followed by the lock
lights and opening balance. A new glass accent connects the arrival to the
soft farewell. The balance still reveals at 620ms and the first chamber is
ready at 1.2s; nothing adds a wait or starts the next viewer automatically.

Each earned +1 unlock now visits a single-door special vault after the
configured starting chambers are finished. Rose Jewel, Candy Treasury and
Moonlit Crown rotate within each round, starting from a variation derived
from its saved id. An early +1 leaves the main vault in place. Each design
has its own closed/open treasure atlas, a stationary frame, two clipped
hinged leaves, centered prize/coin paths and a short arrival phrase.
Reconnects retain the same design and consume old sounds. The final special
vault closes with the existing farewell; a new viewer returns to the Crown.

The 420×460 display stays transparent with the same readable total. Prizes,
odds, counts, bonus guarantee, exact totals, saved settings, volume and the
ending hold/clear times are unchanged. Reduced motion uses stationary
entrance fades and a single change to the next vault at readiness.

Art tour: choose **Special vault · Rose Jewel / Candy Treasury / Moonlit Crown**
and **Play special vault**. The timeline also shows the preceding crown
chamber and its handoff. `scene=reset-2` shows the farewell and next viewer.
In the game, **Sound on** and **Next demo bonus → +1 unlock** let Faith hear
and try it without changing a saved round. Special art only arrives once
the starting chambers have been played.

`public/assets/vault/bonus-{rose,candy,moon}-v17.png`. The approved Crown
