# Four Darts game ranges

Open your saved Darts widget in the editor. Under **Final-total ranges**, give
each of the four games a name, minimum total and maximum total, then select
**Save changes**. You do not need to price individual balloons.

The starting examples are Low ($100–$300), Medium ($400–$600), High ($800–$1,200)
and Extreme ($1,500–$2,500). Rename them and change any limits to suit Faith.
Use whole-dollar limits from $1 to $1,000,000.

## Play on stream

1. Open the widget’s private controls and enter the viewer’s name.
2. Choose **Start** on one of the four game buttons. That click starts the round
   and throws the first dart.
3. Click **Throw next dart** (or Space outside a field) for each remaining dart.
   Extra darts appear automatically. The final total and celebration play after
   the last dart, then the display clears for the next viewer.

The chosen range includes gold, all multipliers and extra darts. Balloon cash
values and a valid sequence are prepared before the first throw; each hit pays
the amount shown, and every bonus is applied normally. There is no hidden change
to the final total. Different rounds can take different paths through the bonuses.

All four games use the widget’s starting dart count and bonus settings. They use
a standard five-colour cash bouquet and standard cash draw proportions instead
of the optional custom cash list. The bonus within the first three darts still
applies under the existing bonus rules. Classic dartboards also support ranges.
Very narrow exact totals can be impossible with restrictive bonus settings; the
game reports this before starting. Widen that range or enable another bonus.

A saved username guarantee takes priority over any of the four game ranges.
The private notice shows which total range applies. It never changes halfway
through the round, even if settings are edited or the page is refreshed.

Only one round can run for this widget at a time. All four start buttons stay
disabled until its total clears. They share the same existing transparent
540 × 540 display link; no extra Browser Sources are needed. The stream display
receives the visible balloons and awards, not the private ranges or future draws.

## Try each game

Select **Demo** on any range card to watch a complete automatic sample with its
bonuses and extra darts. In the editor, demos use the current edits even before
saving. The **Demo game** menu also lets you choose a game and test one dart at a
time or play a full demo. Enter a saved demo username to test its override.

**Stop demo** finishes the current dart; **Reset demo** clears the sample.
From private controls, Demo opens a separate editor tab and keeps the active
stream round untouched. Forced-bonus shortcuts remain available under
**Custom amounts** with the demo username blank.

Old custom amounts are preserved under **Custom amounts & odds · optional**.
Use **Play with custom amounts → Start custom game** on the controls to play them.
Saved widgets, duplicates and settings backups include the four ranges. Older
backups receive the editable starter ranges without altering their existing prizes.
Settings stay in the same browser; download a backup before changing computers.
