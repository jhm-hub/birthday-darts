FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

Final-total ranges

Set a name and final total for each private start button. Balloon values are created automatically, with gold, multipliers and extra darts included in the range. All four games use your starting darts and birthday bonus settings below.

Range totals favor the lower end: about 75% in the lower half. Higher totals remain possible. If both limits are the same, the total stays exact.

Demo plays a full sample using your edits. Save changes to update the live buttons. A saved username range takes priority for that viewer.

Only the balloon you hit stays popped. Other balloons with the same amount remain eligible. The bouquet resets for each viewer.

Birthday bonuses

A bonus within the first three throws. Gold boosts future cash hits; the multiplier applies to the whole final total.

Extra darts respect the round limit and the remaining balloons. Cash and bonus odds share the draw. The guarantee applies to games with at least 3 starting darts. Popped balloons stay gone. Gold stacks add to the original value: the default is +10%, then +20%.

Changes apply to the next round.
A round already playing keeps its original rules.

OFF STREAM · RECURRING

Username ranges

Give a viewer a final total within a range, every time they play this saved game. Their round’s cash values are prepared before the first dart, including gold and multipliers. These rounds replace the usual cash amounts and random prize draws.

Choose Demo beside a saved username to watch their full round. Names ignore capitals and an initial @.

Changes apply to future rounds. An active round keeps its prepared values. Ranges stay in this browser and are included in settings backups.
