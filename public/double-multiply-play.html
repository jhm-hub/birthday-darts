Line landings

When enabled, each wheel independently uses this chance to land between two spots. Their two numbers are added for that wheel, then used in the Add or Multiply result. Starts at 25%; change it whenever you like. Turning this off keeps all landings at spot centres. Changes apply to the next viewer.
