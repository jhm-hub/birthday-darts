Ready for your stream.

No download or account needed.

Open the game ↗
  1. Choose your game. Open Birthday Darts in your usual browser and select Birthday balloons.
  2. Set your prizes. Edit the amounts, odds and number of darts. The preview changes as you type. Click Throw demo dart once per dart to try it, or Reset demo to start over. These demos only appear on this page. Click Save changes when you’re happy.
  3. Open your controls. Click Open controls, then copy the Private display link. Keep this control tab open while streaming.
  4. Add it to your stream. In OBS or Streamlabs, go to Sources → + → Browser / Browser Source. Name it “Birthday Darts”, paste the display link into the URL box, and set Width: 720 and Height: 540. Save, then move or resize it where you want it in your scene. Keep this source above your camera/background in the Sources list. Transparency is automatic.
  5. Try a test round. Back in the control tab, enter “Test” and click Throw dart. The game appears on stream. Click once per dart; it shows the final total and disappears automatically. A blank display between rounds is normal.
  6. Check the pop sound. Turn Sound off on the control page to mute its preview, then check the Browser Source’s sound in your stream mixer or a short recording. The separate stream display still plays its pop sound.

Keep the control tab open and your complete display link private. Use the same browser each time. Your games save there; use Download settings backup before changing computers.

For smoother scene switching, leave Shutdown source when not visible and Refresh browser when scene becomes active off in the Browser Source settings. If you choose the classic dartboard instead, use 720 × 450.
