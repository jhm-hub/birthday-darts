The twelve cash slices

Gift Grab

Two hidden presents. One contains each amount, shuffled every time. Faith chooses left, right or random for the viewer.

Cake or Crumbs

A fair 50/50 reveal: a little crumb or a birthday surprise.

Double Damage

Adds the previous spin’s amount again. Consecutive repeats are allowed. On spin one, use the amount below.

The rules, at a glance
  • Exactly three spins. Every slice has a 1 in 15 chance, every time.
  • Each special slice uses one spin. Gift Grab’s pick does not use another spin.
  • Double Damage repeats the previous spin’s amount, including a special reveal. It does not double the whole round.
  • All amounts add to the round total. This game does not collect payments.

Faith’s original setup averages $24.98 per round. About 48.8% of rounds land a special; about 9.7% directly reveal the $30 cake surprise.
