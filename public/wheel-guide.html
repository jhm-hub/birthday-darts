Before the first turn

Choose a saved Regular or High Roller setup, check its values and special slots, then save. Save as another prototype makes a named copy. Each setup keeps its own numbers, colours, font and PNG.

Under Her signature centre, use Upload centre PNG to add Faith’s transparent PNG and adjust its diameter. The preview trims transparent padding and centres the visible artwork. The image stays upright while rings spin or bonus wheels appear. Click Save prototype to retain it after a refresh.

Use Start turn for random play. The labelled showcase button demonstrates the mechanic with staged outcomes and temporary special slots. Use showcases when explaining the game; the host flows below describe normal play.

Keeping the show clear

Announce the rules that matter before starting: the preset, spin allowance, collection target or pot. During play, name the choice or unlock as it happens. At the end, read the final game result and leave a beat for the reaction.

These examples are starting points for Faith’s presentation. She can change values and styling between turns, while the current turn keeps its starting setup.
